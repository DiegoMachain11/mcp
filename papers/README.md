# Dairy Science Papers

Place PDF or plain-text (`.txt`) research papers in this directory.
After adding files, run the ingestion script to index them:

```bash
cd /Users/diego/Documents/mcp
python -m rag.ingest
```

## Recommended papers to add

### Transition Cow / Health
- LeBlanc et al. (2006) - Metabolic predictors of displaced abomasum in dairy cattle (JDS)
- Duffield et al. (2009) - Impact of hyperketonemia in early lactation dairy cows (JDS)
- Goff & Horst (1997) - Physiological changes at parturition (JDS) — milk fever, hypocalcemia
- Ingvartsen (2006) - Feeding and management-related diseases in periparturient dairy cow (Livestock Sci)

### Fertility / Reproduction
- LeBlanc (2010) - Monitoring reproductive performance in dairy herds (Vet Clin Food Anim)
- Royal et al. (2000) - Genetic and phenotypic relationships of fertility with body condition (Livestock Prod Sci)
- Wiltbank et al. (2006) - Progesterone effects on anovulation and reproductive efficiency (JDS)

### Production / Nutrition
- NRC (2001) - Nutrient Requirements of Dairy Cattle, 7th edition (key chapters)
- Bauman & Currie (1980) - Partitioning of nutrients during pregnancy and lactation (JDS)
- Berchmans & Hermans (2008) - Relationship between body condition score change and energy balance (JDS)

### Calf Management
- Godden et al. (2019) - Colostrum management for dairy calves (Vet Clin Food Anim)
- Drackley (2008) - Calf nutrition from birth to breeding (Vet Clin Food Anim)

### Herd Management / Culling
- Hadley et al. (2006) - Optimum voluntary waiting period for US Holstein herds (JDS)
- Pinedo & De Vries (2010) - Effect of herd size and milk production on involuntary culling (JDS)

## File naming convention
Name files descriptively so metadata is clear, e.g.:
  `leblanc_2006_displaced_abomasum_metabolic_predictors.pdf`
  `duffield_2009_hyperketonemia_early_lactation.pdf`
